Ruling: `learnedThisWeek` was wrong as planned. Counting known words by `lastSeen` means a word learned months ago but reviewed yesterday is counted as learned this week — and at steady state nearly every known word gets reviewed, so the figure would creep toward the total and claim "+147 this week" when nothing new was learned. Amended the plan: `Progress` gains `knownOn: string | null`, stamped once by `applyAnswer` the day a word first crosses in both directions, and `summarise` counts that instead. This also moves `isKnown` and the threshold into `leitner.ts`, because `applyAnswer` must ask the question to stamp the date and the module recording answers must not import the one deriving statistics. Words already known before the migration get `knownOn: null` — we genuinely do not know when they were learned, and guessing would inflate the figure on the first launch after upgrading. Cost if wrong: one nullable field and four tests.
